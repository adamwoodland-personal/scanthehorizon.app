What can you see from the Glass House Mountains Lookout?

From the Glass House Mountains Lookout, Scan the Horizon names 25 peaks and hills within 25 km. The tallest close by is Mount Beerwah (556 m), 4.0 km to the north-north-west, and the coast at Caloundra is 27 km away.

Look from here yourself

Open the lookout in Scan the Horizon and it turns with your phone, or drag to look around on a computer. Tap a name for its height, a link to Wikipedia, and Go there, to see the view from that peak. At the lookout itself, tap Use my location instead.

Visiting the lookout

According to Queensland Parks, the Glass House Mountains lookout day-use area is in Beerburrum West State Forest, with spectacular viewpoints over Glass House Mountains National Park and a short walk; facilities include picnic tables and toilets for people using wheelchairs. The national park is about 70 km, or an hour's drive, north of Brisbane.

The mountains have high spiritual significance for local Indigenous people, and Queensland Parks asks visitors to be considerate and use only the walking tracks and lookouts provided (About the park).

Questions

Which is the tallest peak near the Glass House Mountains Lookout?

Mount Beerwah, 556 m (its official height), 4.0 km to the north-north-west of the lookout. It is the tallest that Scan the Horizon names within 10 km of it.

How far is the lookout from the coast?

Caloundra, on the coast, is 27 km away to the east-north-east, and Bribie Island 23 km to the east. Scan the Horizon shows both only just in view over the ridges in between.

Are the heights and distances exact?

The distances are worked out from the lookout to each place. Heights marked official are GeoNames’ figures; the others are read from the elevation data, which can come out a little low on a sharp peak. Whether something far away just shows over a ridge can change with the weather, because the air bends light more on some days than others.
