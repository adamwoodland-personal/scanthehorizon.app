Peak finder apps compared

Four tools name the mountains on your horizon, and each is best at something different. PeakFinder is a polished offline app, PeakVisor adds 3D hiking maps, HeyWhatsThat is a long-running free website, and Scan the Horizon names towns, islands and airports as well as peaks, free in a browser. Peakery, often searched alongside them, is a log of the peaks you climb.

What each one is best at

PeakFinder

A paid app with no recurring costs that knows over a million peaks and works completely offline, worldwide, with no account. Its camera view overlays the drawing on the picture, and “fly up” shows what lies behind a ridge. Choose it for remote trips with no signal at all.

PeakVisor

A hiking and skiing app as much as a peak finder: 3D maps with huts and ski resorts, live camera labels on peaks and passes, and an Apple Watch app. The app is free to download; the camera view and offline 3D maps need PRO.

HeyWhatsThat

A free website that computes the summits visible from any spot in the world, and draws a map of all the ground you can see from there. Its servers do the work, so it needs a connection.

Peakery

Not a horizon app: a free log and community for the peaks you climb, with Peak Challenges and 645,000 peaks you can browse even offline. None of its pages describes a camera or horizon view.

Where Scan the Horizon differs

  • More than peaks. It names the towns, islands, lakes, lighthouses and airports on your horizon as well as the hills, each with how far away it is.
  • What the hills hide. A big town behind a hill isn’t left off: it gets an arrow over the hill that hides it.
  • Nothing to install. It is free, runs in any browser on a phone or a computer, and has no account.
  • Offline when you plan for it. Save an area up to 200 km across before you lose signal; elsewhere it needs a connection, which PeakFinder doesn’t.

It also has limits worth knowing: names come from GeoNames, so a small hill that isn’t there isn’t named, and a phone compass is good to a few degrees, so you may need to drag the drawing to line it up.

Questions

Is there a free app that names the mountains you can see?

Scan the Horizon is free and runs in your phone’s browser, with nothing to install and no account. HeyWhatsThat is a free website. PeakVisor’s app is free to download, but its camera view needs a PRO subscription, and PeakFinder is a one-off purchase.

Which peak finder works offline?

PeakFinder works completely offline, worldwide. PeakVisor works offline in its app after you download the area, and its offline 3D maps need PRO. Scan the Horizon works offline for views you have already opened and for areas you save ahead, from 2 to 200 km. HeyWhatsThat works out each view on its own servers, so it needs a connection.

Do I need to install an app?

Not for Scan the Horizon or HeyWhatsThat, which run in a web browser. PeakFinder and PeakVisor are phone apps, and both also have a viewer on their websites.
